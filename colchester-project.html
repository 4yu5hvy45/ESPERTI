ColchesterConstruction.

A traditional corner building in Colchester, explored from the retained street facade into the fit-out and services coordination. Scroll to move through the study.

Indicative 3D model, built from drawings and photographs
01 / The corner

The corner,turned.

The upper floors turn the street corner in a curved bay with narrow sash windows, above a painted shopfront.

02 / The upper floors

Brick,kept as found.

Buff stock brick, gauged arches and white sash windows with slim balconettes were retained. The work sits behind them.

03 / The shopfront

Grey,painted timber.

Panelled pilasters, plinths and layered cornices in one grey, with new window graphics across the glazed bays.

04 / The side street

Round thecorner.

The frontage wraps the corner and runs back along the side street, above brick-paved pavement.

05 / After dark

Lit fromwithin.

At dusk the glazing and window graphics glow. The green vinyl lights the pavement. Now, we go inside.

06 / Threshold

07 / Dining

Dining,banquette-led.

A green banquette follows the splayed wall, with loose timber tables, pendants above and a cassette unit over the floor.

08 / Counter

The counter,under the sign.

The order point and menu boards sit between the dining room and the kitchen pass.

09 / Kitchen

Kitchen,east wall.

The cooking line runs along the east wall, with the 600×600 extract riser rising from the rear corner.
